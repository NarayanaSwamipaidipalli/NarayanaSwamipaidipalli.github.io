OUR FIRST EVERYDAY COMPANION

Aveno To-Do

Android 8.0+

An offline-first task manager for work, life and everything in between. No compulsory account. No cloud connection needed for your plans.

01 / PLAN

Start with one small thing.

Capture tasks, add notes and subtasks, and give each intention a deadline, priority or a list of its own.

02 / FIND YOUR RHYTHM

A little structure helps.

Keep a calendar, set repeating tasks and local reminders, and see daily and weekly completion progress.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Your space, your way.

Choose a light or dark theme. Search and filter your lists. Export a readable backup when you want a copy.

Google Play release is in preparation. Local reminder delivery follows Android notification permissions and battery settings.

OFFLINE BY DESIGN

Your plans stay close.

Task data is stored on your device. You can manage your plans without internet access and choose where to save an exported backup.

Optional Google banner ads may load online after applicable privacy checks. Ads can be switched off without losing essential task features.
